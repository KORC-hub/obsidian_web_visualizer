| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| - Permanent Situation<br>- Repeat actions or habits <br>- General truths<br>- tell stories | - Temporaly situation<br>- Situation which are changing <br>- actions, repeat actions or habits, occurring at the moment of speaking |

8. He appears to be vey friendly but I don't know him very well.

9. There's nobody at the door; you are just hearing things. ==(Aquí no se usa Continuous porque _hear_ es un **verbo de percepción (stative verb)** y normalmente no se usa en forma continua en este contexto.)==
